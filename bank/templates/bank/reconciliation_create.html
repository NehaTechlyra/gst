About Bank Reconciliation

Bank reconciliation is the process of matching your bank statement entries with the transactions recorded in LyraERP. This ensures your financial records are accurate and up-to-date.

  • Upload your bank statement as a CSV file
  • System automatically matches entries by date and amount
  • Manually match any unmatched items
